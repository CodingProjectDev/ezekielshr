LITTLE DISCOVERIES — TAP AN ANIMAL TO PLAY ITS GIF

WHAT CHANGED
- Animal picture cards show still previews, not auto-playing animations.
- Each preview comes from the same GIF, so the animal looks the same before
  and during playback.
- Tap a cat, dog, cow, elephant, bird, or sheep to hear its name and play
  its real-motion clip once. Tap again to replay from the start.
- GIFs stop automatically and return to their preview image after roughly
  three seconds (the bird's shorter motion plays for about 1.4 seconds).
- Only one animal moves at a time. Reduced-motion settings prevent playback.
- The previous numbers 1–10 and three-learning-areas updates are included.

HOW TO APPLY
1. Download Little-Discoveries-Tap-to-Play-Animals.zip to your Mac.
2. Open your existing Little-Discoveries-V2 folder in VS Code.
3. Back up any newer changes to app.js, design.css, index.html, and sw.js.
4. In that project's terminal, run:

   unzip -o ~/Downloads/Little-Discoveries-Tap-to-Play-Animals.zip -d .

5. Rebuild the web app:

   npm run build

6. Run tests:

   npm test

7. For an installed iPad PWA, deploy the updated files to your HTTPS host,
   then reopen it online and let the new offline cache finish installing.
   An installed PWA cannot update from files that live only on your Mac.

8. To update the Android project:

   npm run android:sync

WHAT THIS PATCH DOES NOT CHANGE
- First Words and its parent-recorded voice feature remain.
- Little Numbers still shows 1–10.
- Colors & Shapes, Play Together, My Family, and Grown-ups remain available.
- Local family photos, voice recordings, and progress are not deleted.
